# Example findings (synthetic sample)

- **744** mock sign-ins, **161** failed events (21.6%).
- **2** user-day combinations reached at least eight failures and were flagged for review.
- The demonstration deliberately inserts two dense failed-login sequences into otherwise randomly generated events.
- An alert is a **triage indicator**, not proof of an attack, account compromise, or an unsafe geographic region.

Usernames are fictional, and every source network is from documentation-only TEST-NET ranges (RFC 5737).
