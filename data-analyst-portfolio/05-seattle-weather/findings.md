# Findings from historical public observations

- **1461** daily rows spanning **2012-01-01** through **2015-12-31** (2012, 2013, 2014, 2015).
- **623** days recorded >0 mm precipitation, or **42.6%** of observations.
- Warmest month by average daily maximum temperature in this sample: **Aug** (26.1 °C).
- Month with the largest **pooled four-year** precipitation total: **Nov** (642.5 mm). Pooled totals are not annual averages.

The categorical weather label in the source file was synthesized by Vega Datasets. This historical dataset is not a forecast, and four years alone are insufficient for climate-change conclusions.
