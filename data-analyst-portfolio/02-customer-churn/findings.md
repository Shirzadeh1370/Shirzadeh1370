# Example findings (synthetic sample)

- **360** customers, **60** marked churned; overall churn **16.7%**.
- Churn rate with ≥4 recent support tickets: **25%**; with fewer than 4: **12.1%**.
- The association does **not** establish that tickets caused churn; plan mix, tenure, and sample design can affect the comparison.

These rates were intentionally generated for a learning exercise, not estimated from a real subscription business.
